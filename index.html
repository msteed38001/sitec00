Four Operational Leaks Caught by Deep Data Auditing

  • The CA vs. CS Trap: Standard validation sees two valid 2-character Unit of Measure abbreviations. It misses that CA means Case and CS means Cassette. Mixing them up breaks B2B invoicing and stalls the supply chain.
  • Orphaned Inventory: A distributor saw flat sales despite major product line conversions. Sales reps were simply swapping lines to trigger activity KPIs. This created dead, orphaned inventory bleeding 27% in carrying costs.
  • The $40,000 Dust Collection: Found a $75 part purchased in 1983 still on a shelf in 2008. Proved the 27% compound carrying cost math on the remaining dead stock to the president. Saved the company $40,000 annually.
  • The Heavy Manufacturing Blind Spot: Audited a distributor in a heavy manufacturing town where a single automation vendor made up 55% of total sales. Identified they carried zero cutting tools. The data was perfectly clean, but the market strategy was blind.
