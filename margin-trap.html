The Gross Margin Trap

Your ERP says you sold an item at a 25% margin. The G/L says you actually made 4.7%.

This is the most common, silent financial leak in distribution. The sales team thinks they are hitting their targets, the ERP dashboard is green, and the company is slowly going underwater.

I built this short exposé to show exactly how the math hides the carrying cost, and why standard reporting will never catch it.

The Illusion

The ERP system sees a purchase price, a sale price, and calculates the gross profit. It looks fine on the dashboard. But the carrying cost doesn't hit the COGS line. It hides in OpEx.

The Reality

Storage, cost of capital, and obsolescence don't come from one place. They come from three. When you apply them, that 25% margin drops to 4.7%.
