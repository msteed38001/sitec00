CARDex: Your Local Mini-CRM

A lightweight, offline rolodex for independent reps and agents. Get the rich search, filtering, and data modeling of a modern CRM without the cloud, the subscriptions, or the bloat.

The Local Mini-CRM. Built for Speed.

CARDex is a lightweight, offline rolodex for independent reps, agents, and distributors. It gives you the rich search, filtering, and data modeling capabilities of a modern CRM without the cloud, the monthly subscriptions, or the browser bloat.

It runs entirely on your local machine. No internet connection required. No data leaving your laptop.

High-Density Data Entry

When you are managing hundreds of contacts and accounts, scrolling through a web page kills your productivity. CARDex fits 150+ fields onto a single 13-inch screen using the "Cabinet" UI. You navigate entirely by keyboard.

Stacked Search & Filtering

You don't need a cloud database to run complex queries. CARDex uses an in-memory search stack. You can layer filters (e.g., state:TN + company:Acme + status:Lead) instantly, without waiting for a loading spinner.

The Interface

Because the engine is entirely driven by local SQLite databases and TOML configs, you can model the data exactly how your business operates.

Why Local?

Independent reps and small agencies don't need the complexity of a HubSpot or a Salesforce. They need a tool that works on a laptop, even when the internet is down. They need their data to be secure, private, and instant.

CARDex is built on the Micro-Kernel architecture. It handles the SQLite CRUD, the Curses rendering, and the relational lookups locally. If you want to add a new field to track a specific contact detail, you just add it to the TOML file. The Python engine handles the rest.

Does it sync to the cloud?

No. That is the point. Your data lives entirely on your local drive. You don't have to worry about monthly subscription fees, data breaches on a vendor's server, or API rate limits. If you want to back it up, you just copy the .db file to a USB drive or a local network share.

Can I export the data?

Yes. The action plug-in architecture allows you to export your current filtered view to TSV, CSV, or a formatted Markdown table. You can also copy a single record directly to your OS clipboard with a single keystroke.
