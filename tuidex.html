TUIDex: Your Local Knowledge Base

TUIDex is a personal knowledge manager (PKM) built for people who think in data, not just documents.

While other tools force you to write sprawling, unstructured pages, TUIDex lets you model your knowledge into structured tables, link related concepts, and query everything instantly. It is a private, offline database for your brain.

Structured Note-Taking

Notes shouldn't just be a wall of text. TUIDex lets you define custom fields (tags, categories, sources, confidence levels) so you can filter and find exactly what you need without scrolling through endless paragraphs.

The "Cabinet" Detail View

When you open a note, you aren't stuck in a single long document. The data is broken into Cabinets—Basic Info, Links, Content, and Admin. You jump between them instantly with Tab and arrow keys.

The Interface

Why Local?

Cloud-based PKMs like Notion or Evernote are great, until you lose internet access, hit a subscription paywall, or realize your private notes are being used to train an AI model.

TUIDex keeps everything on your local drive. It is fast, private, and permanent. Because it uses the Micro-Kernel architecture, you can customize the fields, create custom actions, and export your data to Markdown at any time. You own the data.

Can I link entries to each other?

Yes. The engine supports relational lookups. You can link a note to a specific project, vendor, or another note. The lookup tables are cached in memory, so the relations render instantly without a network request.

How does it handle long-form content?

For long-form text or raw HTML/Markdown, TUIDex uses a full-screen text editor with horizontal scrolling. You can even press F4 to drop into Notepad to write the content, and the TUI will instantly read it back into the database when you save.
