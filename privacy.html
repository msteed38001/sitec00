Our Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this technical consulting practice handles data. The core philosophy of this business is simple: I do not track you, I do not drop cookies, and I do not collect your data for marketing purposes.

1. No Tracking, Cookies, or Captchas

  • Cookies: This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, local storage, or persistent identifiers.
  • Third-Party Analytics: No third-party tracking scripts, behavior monitoring tools, or user session recorders are active on this site.
  • Automated Verification: This site does not use third-party captcha services or automated challenge-response verification utilities that harvest visitor device telemetry.

2. Information I May Collect

The only information processed is data you explicitly and voluntarily choose to provide:

  • Direct Inquiries: If you contact me directly via email regarding consulting services, data engineering architecture, or project scopes, I will retain your email address, name, and any technical specifications or message context you provide.
  • Purpose: This information is used strictly to communicate with you, evaluate prospective professional assignments, and fulfill my consulting obligations.

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • Security: Direct communications and project requirements are treated as highly confidential and stored securely using standard professional access controls.
  • Retention: Correspondence and project-related telemetry are retained only as long as necessary to conduct active business operations, manage professional client relationships, or satisfy technical auditing and legal obligations.
  • No Commercial Sharing: Your information is never sold, traded, rented, or distributed to third-party brokers, marketers, or external platform networks.

4. Third-Party Interactions

  • External Infrastructure: This site is a static technical deployment. Any server logs managed by the underlying hosting infrastructure only record standard internet telemetry (such as your IP address and request timestamps) necessary to securely serve pages and prevent denial-of-service disruptions. These logs are not cross-referenced with your personal identity.
  • Outbound Links: If you click an external link to a technical resource, repository, or external corporate page, your interaction is governed entirely by that specific asset's own privacy provisions.

5. Your Rights and Contact Information

You retain the right to review, update, or request the deletion of any direct email correspondence or project notes I maintain.
